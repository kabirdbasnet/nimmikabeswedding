Getting There

Fly into Kempegowda International Airport (BLR). The airport is well connected to most Indian cities and major international hubs.

Taxis and app cabs are available at arrivals around the clock. Allow 60–90 minutes for the drive into the city.

Where to Stay

We're arranging a block of rooms close to the venue for out-of-town guests. Details and booking codes are on their way.

If you'd like to book early, hotels in Bengaluru's central neighbourhoods are a good base for the weekend.

FAQ

A few things you might be wondering.

When should I arrive and leave?

Celebrations begin on the morning of June 25 and wrap up after the wedding on June 27. We suggest arriving by June 24 and leaving on June 28 to enjoy everything without rushing.

What should I wear?

Each event has its own mood. Bright festive colours for Haldi & Mehendi, glamorous evening wear for the Sangeet, and traditional formal for the wedding. Dress codes are listed under each event above.

Can I bring a plus one?

Your invitation will note exactly who it's addressed to. If you have a question about a guest, please reach out to us directly.

Are children welcome?

We love your little ones. Please let us know in your RSVP so we can plan seating and meals.

What about gifts?

Your presence is the best present. If you'd like to give something, we'll share details closer to the date.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Formal invitations with full details and the RSVP deadline will follow. For now, save the date and start planning your outfits.
